For Investors

Private lending opportunities backed by real estate

BR Realty offers qualified investors the opportunity to lend capital on value-add real estate projects across the United States.

Our investor model is simple: investors provide capital and receive fixed annual interest, with target returns up to 15% annually, depending on deal structure, term, and risk.

Up to 15% Target annual interest
Nationwide U.S. opportunity focus
Secured Real-estate-backed structure

Investor overview

  • Private debt, not equity partnership
  • No property management responsibilities
  • Clear interest-based return structure
  • Real property security where applicable
  • Designed for qualified investors
Important:

Returns are not guaranteed. All investments involve risk. Any offering should be reviewed with qualified legal and financial advisors.
